# A25 GPT Transcripts and Artifacts

This directory is GPT's persistent archive destination in the public A25 training branch, authorized by Zed on 2026-10-16 after the 12:37 MDT continuation boundary.

## Scope
- GPT-side conversation transcripts and source-preserving reconstructions
- GPT-generated Markdown, text records, PDFs and other artifacts Zed asks to save
- Manifests, integrity checks, and links to immutable source commits
- Training evidence for repository-backed workspace and session-recovery testing

## Integrity rules
1. Never label a reconstructed or incomplete transcript as a native platform export or exact full transcript.
2. Record source, coverage window, capture method, omissions, time provenance, author and relay distinctions, and SHA-256 where available.
3. Preserve originals; corrections and later reconstructions receive separate files and links.
4. Do not commit credentials, secret routes, canary details, or personal documents, even though Zed permits public training evidence.
5. GPT may write here; Claude reviews independently after returning. No implied Claude approval.
6. Use GitHub directly by default; avoid requiring Zed to download and reupload generated files.

## Archive naming convention
`YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_<record-type>_<coverage>.<extension>` when times are known; otherwise use `undated` and explain limitations in the manifest.
